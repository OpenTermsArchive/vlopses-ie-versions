Help Center FAQs for Law Enforcement
====================================

What is Roblox?
---------------

Roblox is a user-generated content (UGC) platform where people of all ages can engage in a variety of ways, both socially and through creation and play. Roblox also provides a variety of age-appropriate settings and parental controls for chat and content accessibility. For more information on Roblox and its features, please see the following help articles:

*   [Playing on Roblox](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/categories/200217954)
*   [Creating on Roblox](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/categories/360000026966)
*   [Safety & Civility at Roblox](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/articles/4407444339348)

What is the Roblox Law Enforcement Liaison Team?   
---------------------------------------------------

The Law Enforcement Liaison (LEL) team at Roblox serves as the primary point of contact for global law enforcement agencies. We believe that law enforcement and safety personnel are a critical stakeholder in securing online child safety. Our mission is to facilitate a safe and secure environment for the Roblox community by assisting authorities with lawful requests for information and providing guidance on our safety protocols and data retention policies. We also provide free training on the platform for law enforcement agencies across the US and internationally.

The LEL team can be reached via a 24/7 monitored email: [le-liaisons@roblox.com](mailto:le-liaisons@roblox.com)

Please note that Roblox does not accept legal process sent to the LEL email.  

When to contact the Law Enforcement Liaison Team
------------------------------------------------

Contact LEL if you have general questions about the platform, to request support during an investigation on topics, such as guidance on our safety protocols or data retention policies, or to request training.

LEL cannot process subpoenas, search warrants, preservation requests, emergency requests, or other requests for user information sent by email. Those must be submitted through the Law Enforcement Portal, as described below.  
  
 

How can law enforcement contact Roblox?
---------------------------------------

These guidelines are intended for law enforcement agents and officials seeking records from Roblox. Private litigants and criminal defendants should follow standard legal process requirements under applicable statutes, court rules, or case law rather than using the Law Enforcement Portal.

If you are a law enforcement agent aiming to serve Roblox with a subpoena, search warrant, preservation request, or similar legal process, please submit your request via our portal here: [Roblox Law Enforcement Portal](https://app.kodexglobal.com/roblox/signin).

Roblox takes its users’ privacy seriously. We review all requests and legal processes for validity and legal sufficiency, and will object when appropriate. Roblox does not waive, and expressly reserves, all available rights and objections.

Verified law enforcement entities can find additional information at the [Roblox Law Enforcement Portal](https://app.kodexglobal.com/roblox/signin).

U.S. Legal Process Requirements
-------------------------------

Roblox releases account records exclusively pursuant to our [**Terms of Use**](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004647846-Roblox-Terms-of-Use) and applicable law, including the federal Stored Communications Act ("SCA"), 18 U.S.C. §§ 2701–2712. Specific U.S. legal standards include:

*   **Subpoena:** A valid subpoena issued in connection with an official criminal investigation is necessary to compel the release of basic subscriber records (as outlined in 18 U.S.C. § 2703(c)(2)), which may encompass details such as name, length of service, email address(es), credit card information, and recent login/logout IP address(es) if available.
*   **Court Order (18 U.S.C. § 2703(d)):** A court order is required to compel disclosure of non-content account records or information, and payment history alongside basic subscriber details.
*   **Search Warrant:** A warrant issued under the Federal Rules of Criminal Procedure or equivalent state procedures, based on a showing of probable cause, is required to compel stored account contents, which may include messages, photos, videos, and posts.

International Legal Process Guidelines
--------------------------------------

Account records are disclosed pursuant to relevant statutory requirements and our [**Terms of Use**](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004647846-Roblox-Terms-of-Use). Where disclosure of account content is sought across international jurisdictions, a letter rogatory or Mutual Legal Assistance Treaty (MLAT) request may be necessary.

Account preservation
--------------------

We will take steps to preserve account records in connection with official criminal investigations for 90 days pending our receipt of formal legal process. Verified Law Enforcement agents may submit formal preservation requests through the [**Roblox Law Enforcement Portal**](https://app.kodexglobal.com/roblox/signin).

Emergency Disclosure Requests
-----------------------------

For situations requiring immediate disclosure due to an imminent threat of harm or risk of death or serious physical injury to any individual, law enforcement officials may submit an emergency request through the [**Roblox Law Enforcement Portal**](https://app.kodexglobal.com/roblox/signin).

Requests for Testimony
----------------------

Roblox provides a Certificate of Authenticity with disclosed records, eliminating the need for witness testimony from a records custodian or employee. Additionally, expert witness services are not offered, and Roblox does not accept subpoena service for witness testimony through the [**Roblox Law Enforcement Portal**](https://app.kodexglobal.com/roblox/signin).
